## 5. Full Description (App Store "Description")

```
TowerDash Bricks is a hand-crafted nonogram (also known as picross or griddler) — a pure logic puzzle where you reconstruct an architect's blueprint, one brick at a time.

Every row and column has number clues that tell you how the bricks are grouped. Use deduction — not luck — to figure out exactly where each brick belongs. Solve the grid and watch the blueprint come to life as a finished structure.

HOW TO PLAY
• Numbers show the runs of bricks in each line, in order.
• Tap a cell to lay a brick — or drag your finger to fill a whole line fast.
• Not sure? Mark a gap with an X. Marking is always free.
• A misplaced brick costs a heart, so think before you build.
• Stuck? Spend a hint to reveal one correct brick.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• 15 hand-designed blueprints, from gentle 5×5 warm-ups to challenging 13×13 grids.
• A built-in interactive tutorial that teaches the rules in under a minute.
• A relaxed, no-timer experience — solve at your own pace.
• Earn coins by solving puzzles and spend them on helpful power-ups and brick skins.
• Six unique brick styles to decorate your finished blueprints.
• Clean "architect blueprint" visuals and a calm soundtrack you can toggle off anytime.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• 100% offline — play anywhere, no internet required.
• No ads. No account or login. No real-money purchases — every coin is earned by playing.
• Single-player, family-friendly logic. Great for a quick brain workout or winding down.

Sharpen your mind, follow the numbers, and rebuild the city brick by brick.
```

## 10. iOS build & submit checklist (run on a Mac)

> The project is configured and Dart-compiles cleanly. Building/signing must be done on
> macOS with Xcode (cannot be done on Windows).

1. `flutter pub get`
2. `cd ios && pod install` (this regenerates Pods without flame/forge2d) then `cd ..`
3. Open `ios/Runner.xcworkspace` in Xcode.
4. Select the Runner target → Signing & Capabilities → set your Team and confirm the
   bundle id `com.towerlab.tower.dash.bricks`.
5. Confirm Info.plist (already set): Display Name = "TowerDash Bricks",
   `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = false`, portrait-locked at runtime.
6. `flutter build ipa --release` (or Product → Archive in Xcode).
7. Upload via Xcode Organizer / Transporter to App Store Connect.
8. In App Store Connect, paste sections 1–6 into the listing, section 7 into
   "App Review Information → Notes", and send section 8 via the Resolution Center.
9. Privacy "Data Collection": select **Data Not Collected**.
